The first release of Segue, a music player for Android: your own music files and TIDAL in one queue, with Last.fm scrobbling, and integration with MediaSync if you use it.

Install segue-1.0.0.apk on the phone (Android 13 or later) and allow All files access when Segue asks. The README covers setting up TIDAL, Last.fm and MediaSync.
