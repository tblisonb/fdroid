The first release of Segue: your own music files and TIDAL in one queue, with Last.fm scrobbling.

- Your library by artist, album, song, folder and playlist, with search.
- Gapless playback, FFmpeg's decoders and ReplayGain volume levelling.
- .m3u8 playlists, shared with desktop players.
- TIDAL search, favorites and playlists, up to 24-bit FLAC.
- Last.fm scrobbling and stats.
- Light, Dark and Black themes, with accent colours.
- With MediaSync: rescans after a sync, and streams the server's tracks.
